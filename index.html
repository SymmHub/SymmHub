SymmHub

A WebGL platform for creating and exploring symmetry-based applications — from wallpaper, frieze, and spherical groups to general hyperbolic orbifolds, reaction-diffusion PDEs, chaotic attractors, and symmetric vector drawing.

Authored by Vladimir Bulatov and Chaim Goodman-Strauss, with assistance from Scott Vorthmann.

Applications

SymPix Interactive

Symmetry from pictures across wallpaper, triangle, orbifold, and color groups.
SymDraw Vector

Vector drawing with live symmetry folding, curved tiles, and SVG export.
SymSim — Gray-Scott PDE

Symmetry from real-time GPU simulation of the Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion PDE.
SymSim — Ginzburg-Landau PDE

Symmetry from GPU simulations of the complex Ginzburg-Landau PDE.
SymChaos Chaos

Symmetry from chaotic iterated attractors (Clifford, De Jong, Conradi, and more).
Minimal Sample Template

Compact reference application showing how to build on the SymmHub engine.
